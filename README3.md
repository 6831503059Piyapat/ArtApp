## 📖 The Concept

**ConsignDesk** is designed to bring harmony to shared art booths. Instead of relying on messy manual records, this web application provides a centralized canvas where booth administrators and artists can manage products, monitor transactions, and review finances with complete transparency.

### 🎯 Core Objectives
*   **Real-time Tracking:** Monitor sales and inventory seamlessly.
*   **Accurate Earnings:** Calculate each supplier's revenue with precision.
*   **Fair Expense Allocation:** Distribute shared costs like booth rental and transport impartially.
*   **Transparent Summaries:** Generate clear sales and financial reports.
*   **Conflict Resolution:** Eliminate manual errors and financial disputes.

### 🎭 The Users
<details>
<summary><b>1. Booth Administrator / Host</b> <i>(Click to expand)</i></summary>

*   Create and manage **Sales Runs**.
*   Manage suppliers, products, and inventory.
*   Invite members and assign access permissions.
*   Monitor sales transactions and log expenses.
*   Review sales summaries and export final reports.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2. Collaborator / Assistant</b> <i>(Click to expand)</i></summary>

*   Monitor product inventory and sales performance.
*   View individual sales records and earnings.
*   Review financial summaries and allocated expenses.
*   Track live sales progress during an active Sales Run.
</details>

It provides a centralized, transparent platform where booth admins and artists can effortlessly manage products, track transactions, and calculate fair revenue sharing and shared expenses.

### 🎯 What We Achieve

- 💰 **Accurate Earnings:** Calculate every supplier's profit perfectly.
- 🧾 **Fair Expense Splitting:** Allocate booth rentals and transport costs automatically.
- 📈 **Crystal Clear Reports:** Generate transparent financial summaries.
- ✅ **No More Drama:** Reduce manual errors and financial disputes.

### 👥 Who Is It For?

👉 **1. Booth Administrator / Host**
> The Maestro! They create Sales Runs, manage suppliers/inventory, invite staff, log expenses, and export the final reports.
